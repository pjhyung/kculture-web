Seoul, the capital of South Korea, is a city that seamlessly blends traditional and modern lifestyles. The MZ generation, which includes Millennials and Gen Z, plays a significant role in shaping the city's culture. This demographic is driving the trends in beauty, fashion, and lifestyle, making Seoul a fascinating destination for foreigners interested in Korean culture.

One of the most notable aspects of Seoul's modern lifestyle is its beauty trends. Korean skincare and makeup have gained worldwide recognition for their innovative products and techniques. The city is home to numerous cosmetic shops and spas, offering a wide range of services and products. For example, the popular **Innisfree** brand offers a variety of natural skincare products, while **Etude House** is known for its colorful and fun makeup collections.

Seoul's fashion districts are also a must-visit for anyone interested in Korean style. The **Myeong-dong** shopping district is one of the most popular areas, featuring a mix of high-end and affordable fashion brands. Another notable district is **Hongdae**, known for its street fashion, indie music scene, and urban art. Visitors can also explore the **Gangnam** area, which offers a more upscale shopping experience.

In addition to its beauty and fashion scenes, Seoul's modern lifestyle is also characterized by its vibrant food culture. The city offers a wide range of traditional and modern dining options, from street food to high-end restaurants. For instance, **Bukchon Hanok Village** is a popular destination for trying traditional Korean tea and desserts.

To experience the best of Seoul's modern lifestyle, visitors can check out the following recommendations:
  * **SMTOWN Coex Artium**: a entertainment complex featuring K-pop merchandise, exhibitions, and live performances
  * **Garosu-gil**: a trendy street in the Sinsa-dong neighborhood known for its boutique shops, cafes, and restaurants
  * **Laneige**: a popular Korean beauty brand offering a range of skincare and makeup products
