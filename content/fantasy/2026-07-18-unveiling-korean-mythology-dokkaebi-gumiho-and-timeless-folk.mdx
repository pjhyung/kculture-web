Korean mythology is a treasure trove of fascinating creatures, legends, and folklore that have captivated the imagination of people for centuries. At the heart of Korean mythology are the dokkaebi, mischievous goblins known for their love of tricks, music, and dance. These mythical creatures are often depicted as short, grotesque beings with a penchant for playing pranks on humans.

Another iconic figure in Korean mythology is the gumiho, a nine-tailed fox with supernatural powers. According to legend, the gumiho possesses the ability to shape-shift, manipulate the minds of others, and bring good fortune or misfortune depending on its intentions. The gumiho is a complex character, embodying both benevolent and malevolent qualities, making it a compelling figure in Korean folklore.

Korean folklore is filled with stories of these mythical creatures, as well as other legendary beings like the dragon-like creature, Yong. These tales have been passed down through generations, often serving as cautionary stories or explanations for natural phenomena. For those interested in delving deeper into Korean mythology, there are several resources available.

The Korean Folk Village in Yongin, Gyeonggi Province, is a great place to start, offering a glimpse into traditional Korean life and folklore. Visitors can explore the village, watch traditional performances, and learn about the country's rich cultural heritage. For a more immersive experience, consider reading *Korean Folk Tales* by Zong In-Sob, a collection of traditional stories that showcase the country's vibrant folklore.

To explore Korean mythology further, watch the popular TV drama *My Love From the Star*, which features a gumiho as one of its main characters. Alternatively, try playing the video game *Blade & Soul*, which draws heavily from Korean mythology and features dokkaebi and gumiho as characters. For a taste of Korean culture, try sampling some traditional snacks, such as *Pepero*, a popular chocolate-dipped cookie stick that is a favorite among locals and visitors alike.
