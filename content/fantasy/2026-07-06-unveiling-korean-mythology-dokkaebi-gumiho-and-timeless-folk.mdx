# Introduction to Korean Mythology
Korean mythology is a fascinating world of magical creatures, supernatural beings, and legendary heroes. At the heart of this enchanting realm are the dokkaebi goblins and the gumiho nine-tailed fox, two of the most iconic figures in Korean folklore. In this article, we will delve into the captivating world of Korean mythology and explore the timeless tales that have shaped the country's cultural heritage.

# Dokkaebi: Mischievous Goblins
Dokkaebi are mythical creatures from Korean folklore that are known for their mischievous and quick-witted nature. These goblins are often depicted as short, grotesque beings with a penchant for playing tricks on humans. Despite their troublesome behavior, dokkaebi are also believed to possess supernatural powers and are sometimes revered as guardians of rural villages.

# Gumiho: The Nine-Tailed Fox
The gumiho is a legendary creature from Korean mythology that is said to possess extraordinary strength, agility, and intelligence. This mythical being is typically depicted as a beautiful woman with nine tails, each representing a different aspect of her power. In some tales, the gumiho is portrayed as a seductress, while in others, she is a symbol of good fortune and prosperity.

# Exploring Korean Folklore
For those interested in learning more about Korean mythology and folklore, there are numerous resources available. The Korean Folk Village in Yongin, Gyeonggi Province, is a great place to start, offering a glimpse into traditional Korean life and culture. Visitors can also explore the National Folk Museum of Korea in Seoul, which features a vast collection of artifacts and exhibits on Korean folklore and mythology.

# Recommendations
For a deeper understanding of Korean mythology and folklore, consider checking out the following titles: *'Korean Folk Tales'* by Zong In-Sob, which offers a comprehensive collection of traditional Korean stories. You can also watch the popular Korean drama *'My Love From the Star'*, which features a gumiho as one of its main characters. Additionally, try the Korean snack *'Dokkaebi Rice Crackers'* for a taste of the country's unique culinary delights.
