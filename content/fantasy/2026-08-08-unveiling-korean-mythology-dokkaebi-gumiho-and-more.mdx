# Introduction to Korean Mythology
Korean mythology is a rich and fascinating world of legends, filled with unique creatures and supernatural beings. At the heart of Korean folklore are the dokkaebi goblins and the gumiho nine-tailed fox, each with its own distinct characteristics and roles in traditional tales.

## Dokkaebi: The Mischievous Goblin
Dokkaebi are known for their mischievous and quick-witted nature. They are often depicted as short, grotesque creatures with a fondness for playing tricks on humans. However, they are also believed to possess magical powers and are sometimes associated with good fortune.

## Gumiho: The Nine-Tailed Fox
The gumiho, or nine-tailed fox, is a shape-shifter with incredible beauty and cunning. According to legend, a fox that lives for a thousand years can transform into a gumiho, possessing great power and intelligence. While often portrayed as seductive and dangerous, the gumiho can also symbolize wisdom and adaptability.

## Exploring Korean Folklore
To delve deeper into Korean mythology, consider visiting the **National Folk Museum of Korea** in Seoul, which offers a comprehensive look at the country's folk traditions and legends. For a more immersive experience, watch the Korean drama **'My Love From the Star'**, which features a gumiho character and explores themes of love, identity, and supernatural power.

## Recommendations for Further Exploration
For those interested in Korean folklore and mythology, here are a few recommendations:
- Read **'Korean Folk Tales: Imps, Ghosts and Fairies'** by James Riordan, a collection of traditional Korean stories that showcase the country's rich mythological heritage.
- Visit the **Bukchon Hanok Village** in Seoul, a traditional Korean village filled with hanok (traditional Korean houses) and offering a glimpse into the country's historical and cultural past.

By exploring Korean mythology and folklore, foreigners can gain a deeper understanding and appreciation of Korean culture, discovering the unique characters, legends, and traditions that make this country so fascinating.
