Korea's traditional culture is a treasure trove of vibrant history, stunning architecture, and meaningful customs. At the heart of this rich heritage lies the Joseon dynasty, which ruled Korea from 1392 to 1910. During this period, the royal family and nobility wore the iconic **hanbok**, a traditional garment characterized by its elegant design and intricate patterns.

One of the most fascinating aspects of Korean traditional culture is the **royal palaces**, which showcase the grandeur and majesty of the Joseon dynasty. **Gyeongbokgung Palace**, the largest and most iconic palace, is a must-visit destination for anyone interested in Korean history. This stunning palace features an impressive array of traditional Korean architecture, including the majestic **Geunjeongjeon Hall** and the beautiful **Hyangwonjeong Pond**.

Korean traditional culture also places great emphasis on **ancestral rites**, which are ceremonies held to honor one's ancestors. These rites are an integral part of Korean society and are still observed today. The **Jongmyo Shrine**, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is the primary location for these ceremonies and offers a unique glimpse into Korea's spiritual heritage.

For those interested in exploring Korean traditional culture, there are several resources available. The **Korean Cultural Heritage Administration** offers a wealth of information on Korea's cultural heritage, including the history of the Joseon dynasty and the significance of ancestral rites. The **National Museum of Korea** is also a great place to learn about Korean history and culture, with a vast collection of artifacts and exhibits.

To experience Korean traditional culture firsthand, consider watching **'Crash Landing on You'**, a popular Korean drama that showcases the country's stunning landscapes and rich cultural heritage. You can also try wearing a **hanbok** at a traditional Korean dress rental shop, such as **Hanboknam**, or visit the **Bukchon Hanok Village**, a charming traditional Korean village filled with hanok (traditional Korean houses).
