Korean traditional culture is a treasure trove of vibrant history, stunning architecture, and meaningful customs. At the heart of this heritage lies the Joseon dynasty, which ruled Korea from 1392 to 1910. During this period, the royal family and aristocrats wore the iconic **hanbok**, a traditional garment that has evolved over time and is still worn today on special occasions.

One of the most fascinating aspects of Joseon culture is the **royal palaces**, which showcase the dynasty's grandeur and architectural prowess. The most famous palace, **Gyeongbokgung**, is a must-visit attraction in Seoul, with its impressive throne hall and beautifully landscaped gardens. Another notable palace is **Changdeokgung**, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and the best-preserved palace of the Joseon era.

Ancestral rites, known as **jesa**, play a significant role in Korean traditional culture. These rituals are performed to honor and pay respects to one's ancestors, and are typically conducted during special occasions such as the **Lunar New Year (Seollal)** and **Chuseok (Korean Thanksgiving)**. To experience these customs firsthand, visitors can attend a traditional **jesa** ceremony at a Korean cultural center or participate in a **hanbok**-making workshop.

For those interested in delving deeper into Korean traditional culture, there are several excellent resources available. The **National Museum of Korea** in Seoul offers a comprehensive introduction to Korean history and culture, with exhibits on the Joseon dynasty and traditional crafts. The **Korean Cultural Heritage Administration** also provides a wealth of information on Korea's cultural heritage, including royal palaces, folk villages, and traditional festivals.

To get a taste of traditional Korean cuisine, try **Bibimbap**, a signature dish made with mixed vegetables, meat, and rice. For a unique shopping experience, visit **Insadong**, a charming neighborhood in Seoul filled with traditional tea houses, street performers, and souvenir shops selling handmade crafts and **hanbok**-inspired clothing.
