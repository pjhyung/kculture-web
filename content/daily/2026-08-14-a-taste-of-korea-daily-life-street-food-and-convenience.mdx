Korean daily life is a unique blend of traditional and modern culture. From bustling streets to convenient stores, Korea has something to offer for everyone. 
### Daily Life
Koreans typically start their day early, with many taking public transportation to work or school. The Seoul Metro is one of the most efficient and extensive subway systems in the world, with over 20 lines and more than 300 stations. 

### Street Food
Korean street food is famous for its variety and flavor. Popular dishes like **Tteokbokki** (spicy rice cakes) and **Hotteok** (sweet pancakes) can be found at street stalls and markets throughout the country. For a unique experience, visit **Myeong-dong Night Market** in Seoul, which offers a wide range of street food and shopping.

### Convenience Store Culture
Convenience stores, known as **CU**, **GS25**, or **7-Eleven**, are an integral part of Korean daily life. They offer everything from snacks and drinks to toiletries and phone chargers. Many Koreans also use convenience stores to pay bills, send packages, and even order food delivery.

### Korean Language Expressions
To fully immerse yourself in Korean culture, learning a few key phrases can go a long way. Here are a few essentials:
* **Annyeonghaseyo** (hello)
* **Gamsahamnida** (thank you)
* **Joheunhamnida** (you're welcome)

For a true Korean experience, try watching **Crash Landing on You**, a popular K-drama that showcases Korean culture and daily life. You can also listen to **BTS**, one of the most famous K-pop groups, to get a feel for the music and energy of the country.
