Korean daily life is a fascinating blend of tradition and modernity. In Seoul, the capital city, you can experience the unique harmony of old and new. From bustling streets to serene parks, every aspect of life in Seoul is infused with the country's rich culture. 

One of the most exciting aspects of Korean culture is its street food. Popular dishes like **Tteokbokki** (spicy rice cakes) and **Hotteok** (sweet pancakes) can be found at street stalls and markets throughout the city. For a truly immersive experience, visit **Myeongdong Night Market**, where you can sample a wide variety of street foods.

Convenience store culture is also an essential part of Korean daily life. Stores like **7-Eleven** and **CU** are open 24/7, offering everything from snacks and drinks to cosmetics and clothing. These stores are not just for convenience; they're also social hubs where friends and family gather to grab a quick bite or meet up.

To navigate Korean daily life, it's helpful to know some basic Korean language expressions. **Annyeonghaseyo** (hello), **gamsahamnida** (thank you), and **joheunhamnida** (excuse me) are all useful phrases to learn. You can also use the **Naver Dictionary** app to look up words and phrases on the go.

For foreigners interested in Korean culture, there are many resources available. **Korean Class 101** is a popular online course that offers lessons for beginners. You can also watch **Korean dramas** like **Crash Landing on You** to get a glimpse into Korean daily life and culture.

Whether you're interested in food, language, or culture, Seoul has something to offer everyone. With its unique blend of tradition and modernity, Korean daily life is an exciting and rewarding experience to explore.
